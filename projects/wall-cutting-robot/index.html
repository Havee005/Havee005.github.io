Overview

Cutting channels into walls for wiring and pipes fills the air with dust that workers end up breathing in, which is a real health risk on construction sites.

This robot automates the job. A worker controls it from a smartphone up to 20 meters away, so the robot carves the channel while the worker stays clear of the dust.

Highlights

  • Smartphone control within a 20-meter range
  • Cuts channels for electrical and plumbing lines
  • Reduces dust exposure and related health risks for workers
