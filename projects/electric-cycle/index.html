Overview

With fuel costs rising, getting around is expensive for delivery workers. We set out to build an affordable, efficient and eco-friendly e-bike for them.

Instead of designing a new frame, we modified a regular bicycle. That kept the cost down while delivering a 60 km range on a full charge and speeds up to 35 km/h.

Highlights

  • Modified from a standard bicycle to keep costs low
  • 60 km range on a single charge
  • Top speed of 35 km/h
  • Tailored to delivery workers
