Overview

Rigid, jointed arms struggle in tight spaces and with delicate objects. Inspired by octopus tentacles and elephant trunks, this continuum robot bends smoothly along its whole length instead.

That flexibility makes this kind of arm suited to minimally invasive surgery and fruit picking. The soft end effector can lift fragile and brittle objects without damaging them.

How it's built

  1. Continuum arm

    Six 3D-printed PLA segments, each 70 mm long. Threaded discs link the segments and let the tendons slide through, so the arm bends without changing length.

  2. Driving box

    Six servo motors pull 12 thin steel tendons through a pulley mechanism, two tendons per segment.

  3. Control

    An Arduino Uno reads a joystick and talks to a PCA9685 PWM servo controller over I2C, which sets each servo's angle.

  4. Soft gripper

    The end effector is cast from two-part liquid silicone rubber in a 3D-printed mold, cured for 6 hours, and actuated by air.
