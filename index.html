About Me

LET YOUR INNOVATION MEET YOUR DREAM!

It began with a simple, stubborn idea I couldn’t shake — the thought of turning my old RC car into something that could sense its surroundings and move on its own, which is what first pulled me toward robotics in a real way. That early spark of curiosity didn’t fade with time; instead, it slowly deepened into a genuine fascination with how machines perceive the world, decide what to do, and act within it.

Over the years, that fascination has taken shape through hands-on projects, academic research, and long stretches of learning alongside people who pushed me to think differently, which is how I found my way into the worlds of perception, control, and autonomy — three ideas that, together, are what let a machine actually understand and move through its environment. Working across these areas has taught me that engineering well isn’t just about getting something to function, but about getting it to hold up when the world doesn’t behave the way you expect it to, and that lesson has stayed with me through every project since.

That’s ultimately what drives me now: the belief that robotics, done thoughtfully, can quietly make everyday life safer and more efficient, often without anyone noticing the complexity behind it. In many ways, I’m still that kid who wanted his RC car to think for itself — just with more math, more patience, and a lot more still left to learn.
